Short Bio

Zhao-Wei Wang is a PhD student at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Yu-Hong Dai. Before this, he received the Bachelor degree (2023) in mathematics at School of Xuteli, Beijing Institute of Technology. His recent work focuses on linear programming, mixed integer programming, and algorithm design of optimization solvers.
